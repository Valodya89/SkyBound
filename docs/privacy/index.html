Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Last updated 16 September 2026

Starlane does not collect your data. It has no user accounts, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

This policy explains what that means in practice, and describes the two narrow cases where information leaves your device — both of which are handled by Apple, not by us.

The short version

We never see your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your device identifiers or your payment details. Your game progress is a file on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no server to delete it from.

Information we collect

None. Starlane collects, stores and transmits no personal information. We have declared "Data Not Collected" across every category on the app's App Store privacy label, and the app ships with a privacy manifest that says the same thing.

Specifically, Starlane does not:

  • ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal detail;
  • request or access your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera or health data;
  • read your photo library;
  • collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and it therefore never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • include any analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or advertising SDK;
  • track you across other apps or websites, for our benefit or anyone else's.

Information stored on your device

Everything the game knows about you stays on your iPhone, in the app's own private storage:

WhatWhereLeaves your device?
Game progress A JSON file in the app's Application Support directory — scores, coins, gems, level, rockets, upgrades, season progress, daily streak and settings No
Purchase token A random identifier in the app's own preferences, created on this install Only to Apple, with a purchase — see below

None of this is transmitted anywhere. You can erase all of it at any time from Settings ▸ Reset all progress inside the game, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Starlane offers optional in-app purchases: gem packs, a Piggy Bank, Remove Ads, a Founder's Bundle and the VIP Pass subscription. These are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

  • We never receive your payment details. Your card, your Apple Account and your billing address are handled by Apple and are never visible to the app or to us.
  • When you make a purchase, the app sends Apple a randomly generated identifier (an app account token) created on your device when you first launched the game. It is not derived from your name, your Apple Account or any hardware identifier, and it is not linked to your identity. Its only purpose is to let a purchase be matched back to this installation of the game if a payment problem ever needs investigating.
  • Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymous sales and subscription reports — totals by product and by country. These contain no information about individual purchasers.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this one.

Advertising

Starlane shows no real advertisements. The "ad breaks" and rewarded-video moments in the game are a placeholder screen drawn by the app itself and labelled Simulated ad. No advertising network is integrated, nothing is downloaded, nothing is installed, and no information about you is shared with anyone for advertising purposes.

Leaderboards and opponents

The weekly ranking board and the duel opponents are generated on your device by the game itself. They are not real players and no data is exchanged with anyone. The game states this on the Ranks screen.

Sharing a run card

After a run you can share a score card using the standard iOS share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice and happens inside iOS — we receive no copy and no record of it. If you choose Save Image, iOS will ask your permission to add that one image to your photo library. Starlane can only add that image; it cannot read your photos.

Children

Starlane is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to disclose, sell or delete. Parents who want to prevent in-app purchases can use Screen Time ▸ Content & Privacy Restrictions on the device.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete and port the personal data a company holds about you, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct, transfer or erase, and we have never sold or shared personal information — we have none to sell. The data on your device is under your sole control and is removed by resetting your progress or deleting the app. If you would like this confirmed in writing, contact us using the details below.

Data retention and security

We operate no servers and store no user data, so there is no retention period and no database that could be breached. The data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and by your device passcode and encryption.

Third parties

The only third party involved in Starlane is Apple, which distributes the app and processes purchases. We use no other service providers, and we share information with no one because we have none to share. These web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting them is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. They set no cookies, run no analytics and load no third-party resources.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Starlane changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
