Support

Starlane for iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Something not working, or a question about a purchase? Start here — and if the answer isn't below, write to us.

Contact

Email: you@example.com

We read every message and usually reply within two business days. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps a lot. You can also report a bug at github.com/Valodya89/SkyBound/issues.

How to play

GestureAction
Swipe left or right, or tap a sideChange lane
Swipe upClimb over debris
Swipe downDive under a laser gate

A jump or slide you ask for while already in the air is remembered and fires the moment you land, so you can chain two obstacles without waiting. Leaving a lane is instant — you only collide with the lane you have actually reached.

Purchases

I paid for something and didn't get it.
Open Shop ▸ Purchases ▸ Restore (or Settings ▸ Restore purchases). This brings back the VIP Pass, Remove Ads and the Founder's Bundle on any device signed into the same Apple Account. If a gem pack was charged but not delivered, the game re-checks unfinished purchases every time it launches — reopen the app once, and if the gems still haven't arrived, email us with the date and amount.
I reinstalled the app and my purchases are gone.
Tap Restore as above. Non-consumables and the subscription come back. Consumables — gems you already spent — do not, because they were already delivered.
How do I cancel the VIP Pass?
VIP Pass is an auto-renewable subscription at $6.99 per month, billed to your Apple Account. Cancel it in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage VIP in the game's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge; you keep VIP until the period you have paid for runs out.
What happens when VIP ends?
Double coins, the +2 energy capacity and the ad-free experience stop. Everything you earned while subscribed — gems, coins, rockets, season progress — is yours to keep.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We have no ability to issue or deny a refund, but tell us what went wrong so we can fix it.
The shop is empty or prices won't load.
This is almost always a connection problem — the App Store needs to reach Apple to fetch prices even though the game itself works offline. Check your connection and reopen the app; the game retries on its own. If it persists, sign out and back into the App Store in iOS Settings.

Progress and data

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is saved locally on your iPhone and is included in your encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud or Finder). There is no account system and no cloud save, so progress does not sync between devices — restoring an iPhone backup carries it across, signing into another iPhone does not.
I lost my progress.
If the save file is ever unreadable, the game sets it aside rather than overwriting it and starts fresh, so nothing is destroyed. Email us before reinstalling — reinstalling deletes it for good.
How do I delete everything?
Settings ▸ Reset all progress (tap twice to confirm) erases your save. Deleting the app removes it entirely. We hold no copy — see the Privacy Policy.

About the game's systems

What are the crate odds?
65% Common, 25% Rare, 8% Epic, 2% Legendary. A ×10 opening guarantees at least one Rare or better, and the 40th opening without a Legendary is guaranteed to be one — the counter toward that is shown on the crate screen. Duplicates refund coins. Every rocket is cosmetic and none of them make you faster or safer.
Are the leaderboard rivals real people?
No. The weekly board and the duel opponents are generated on your device. The game says so on the Ranks screen. Starlane has no network connection of any kind.
Are the ads real?
No. They are a placeholder screen drawn by the game and labelled "Simulated ad". Nothing is downloaded and nothing is installed. Remove Ads removes these breaks.
How does energy work?
Each run costs energy, which refills over time whether or not the app is open. The base capacity is 5, you can buy capacity up to 12, and VIP adds 2 on top. You can also refill instantly with gems.
Does it need an internet connection?
Only to load shop prices and complete a purchase, both of which are Apple's systems. The game itself — every mode, the Daily Challenge, crates, the season pass — works fully offline.

Accessibility and comfort

Music, sound effects and haptics can each be turned off in Settings. The game is portrait-only and playable one-handed. If something about the game is hard to read or hard to control, tell us — that feedback is genuinely useful and we would rather hear it than not.

Reporting a bug

The most useful report includes:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you were doing when it happened;
  • whether it happens every time or just once;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can get one.

Send it to you@example.com or open an issue on GitHub.
