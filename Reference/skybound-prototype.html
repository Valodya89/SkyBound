ABOUT THIS BUILD
Skybound is an AI-generated game prototype. Every part of it — the concept, game design, 3D renderer, obstacle patterns, boss logic, UI system, artwork, copy and monetization model — was written by Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, from a short text prompt. There is no human-authored art or engine here.

Everything economic in this build is simulated: coins, gems, energy, the storefront, the subscription, the season pass, the crates, the duels, the leaderboards and the "ads" are all local fakes. No payment processor, no ad network and no server are involved.

Progress saves to your own device only. It exists as a demonstration of a complete free-to-play arcade loop, and as a readable example of the retention and monetization patterns the mobile games industry actually uses.
